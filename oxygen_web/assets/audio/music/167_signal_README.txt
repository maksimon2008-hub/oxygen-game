OXYGEN // 167 SIGNAL MUSIC

Place your generated final transmission track in this folder with this exact filename:

    167_signal.ogg

The game switches to this looping track after the one-time "167 SIGNAL DETECTED"
sequence has completed. It continues while the player explores the unlocked
167 archive and Safehouse. Leaving the Safehouse for a campaign sector restores
the normal sector soundtrack.

Format: OGG Vorbis (.ogg)
Suggested loudness: keep the integrated level close to the other OXYGEN music.
The track is optional; until it is added, the regular Safehouse theme plays.

Do not rename the file unless the filename is also changed in oxygen_web/main.py.
